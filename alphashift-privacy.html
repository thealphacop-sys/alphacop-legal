AlphaShift Privacy Policy

AlphaShift: Police Scheduling · Published by Alpha Cop LLC · Effective November 26, 2026

Summary: AlphaShift does not collect, store, or share your personal information. Everything you enter stays on your device.

Information we collect

None. AlphaShift has no user accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking. We do not run any servers for the app, so the information you enter — your rotation, shifts, overtime, pay rates, time-off balances, shift swaps, training classes, and notes — is never sent to us. It is stored only on your device using Apple's standard on-device storage.

If your device backs up to iCloud, Apple may include the app's data in those backups under Apple's own privacy policy.

Calendar access

If you turn on calendar sync, AlphaShift asks for permission to add events to a calendar you choose (for example, a calendar you share with your family). AlphaShift only writes your own schedule into that calendar and only changes or removes the events it created. Calendar information is not sent to us. You can turn calendar access off at any time in your iPhone's Settings.

Purchases

AlphaShift is currently free. If optional subscriptions or purchases are offered in the future, they will be processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never receive your payment information; Apple only tells the app whether a purchase is active.

Children

AlphaShift is intended for adults. It does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children under 13.

Deleting your data

Deleting AlphaShift from your device permanently removes all of its data from that device.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will post the updated version on this page and update the effective date above.
