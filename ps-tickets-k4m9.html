Patrol Summons

Electronic tickets, Special Form complaints, warnings and court delivery for New Jersey police, on any phone, tablet or MDT.

This demo runs on three fictional towns, Anytown, Lakeview and Millbrook, with made-up officers and drivers. Nothing you do is saved.

Built by a New Jersey police officer · Alpha Cop

That's Patrol Summons

Title 39 and parking tickets, Special Form (SC) complaints for ordinances and DP / PDP offenses, plus written warnings
Scan the license and VIN and the ticket fills in. No state connection needed
Checks every summons before it's issued: elements, points, court-mandatory, duplicates
History across every department on the system, with chat between towns
Summonses delivered to the municipal court's inbox the moment they're issued
Supervisor-approved voids, court notes, evidence lists and discovery packets
Roadside release (ROR) forms, monthly recaps and a full audit trail
Runs on any phone, tablet or MDT browser. Nothing to install
